# Project Reclaim marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Solana wallet educators and users with old token accounts. **Offer.** Help wallet owners understand the documented rent-recovery fee and preview before trying the service.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show one fictional quote with separate gross rent, fee, network costs and net; link provider-review status only when verified. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Inspect the fee example. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 users correctly identify fee and net. |

### Sales test

Revenue goal is one reconciled service-fee receipt once the release is approved for the intended wallet. Report network fees and user refunds separately. Count only reconciled service fees from confirmed real reclaims after wallet review and source parity.

### Measurement and decision

Use `reclaim-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Public engine and verification source recovered from the original private repository. Mainnet operation, support review and live wallet compatibility remain unverified. No transaction or wallet action was performed. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
